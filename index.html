YOUR FIRST VISIT

What happens at your consultation?

During your visit
  1. Share your history
  2. Review your scalp and hair
  3. Discuss next steps

1. Share your history

Tell us when you noticed thinning, which areas have changed and what you’ve already tried. Bring older photos or a list of treatments you have tried, if you have them.

2. Review your scalp and hair

Discuss your hair loss pattern and, if you’re considering a transplant, whether you have enough donor hair. Ask what the clinician looks for in the donor area and what coverage may be realistic for you.

3. Decide on a next step

Use the consultation to decide whether to explore treatment, seek another opinion or take more time. Before deciding, ask about estimated costs, time away from work and follow-up.

PATIENT GUIDE

Know what to ask before you decide.

A hair transplant moves hair follicles from a donor area to areas of thinning. Bring these questions to your consultation to understand what surgery could realistically achieve for you.

Read the NHS hair transplant guide ↗

Am I a candidate for a hair transplant?

Ask what is causing your hair loss, whether you have enough donor hair and which alternatives are worth considering. Ask how future thinning could affect the hairline plan.

Who will perform my procedure?

Ask for the surgeon’s name, registration and experience. Find out who will carry out each part of the procedure and who you can contact during recovery.

What is FUE, and what are the risks?

Follicular unit extraction (FUE) removes small groups of hair follicles individually for transplant. Ask how FUE compares with other options and how the team manages risks such as bleeding, infection, scarring and grafts failing to grow.

What should I expect during recovery?

Some swelling and scabbing are common early on. Transplanted hairs can shed before regrowing, and results develop over many months. Ask for an aftercare plan, follow-up schedule and guidance on when to contact the clinic.

What is included in the quote?

Ask for a written estimate that explains the proposed graft count, what the procedure includes and whether follow-up or medication costs are separate.
